❓ How Streak Guardian Works

🌱

Consistency > Intensity

Track 1 to 3 micro-habits daily. Micro-habits are intentionally small so you can maintain streaks even on tough days.

👾

Pet Mood Evolution

Your Guardian's stage reflects your overall average streak strength:

  • Sleepy 0-day avg streak (Missed days)
  • Okay 1-2 day avg streak (Getting started)
  • Happy 3-6 day avg streak (Building momentum)
  • Excited 7-11 day avg streak (1 week strong!)
  • Thriving 12+ day avg streak (Legendary status ✨)
🛡️

Fair Drop System

Skipping a day reduces your average streak health, but doesn't punish you instantly to zero if you had a strong streak. Jump back in immediately to boost your pet!
